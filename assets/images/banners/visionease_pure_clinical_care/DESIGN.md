## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Narrative
The brand embodies optical precision, therapeutic relief, and effortless digital commerce. Guided by the slogan *"Curated Clarity, Crafted for You"*, the visual language bridges clinical ophthalmological credibility with everyday lifestyle comfort. It does not feel sterile like a hospital, nor chaotic like a high-intensity discount retailer. Instead, it offers an eye-resting sanctuary: crisp, balanced, and instantly reassuring to screen-fatigued eyes.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Digital knowledge workers, gamers, students, and post-procedure optical patients seeking certified blue-light lenses, sterile artificial tears, hot/cold thermal therapy masks, and soothing gel packs.
- **Emotional Response:** Physical and cognitive decompression, unshakeable trust in medical-grade certifications, absolute optical legibility, and seamless purchasing confidence.

### Design Aesthetic: Clinical Clarity & Optical Comfort
The visual direction fuses **Modern Clinical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Eye-Care Softness**:
- **Glare-Free Surfaces:** Elimination of pure harsh stark white (#FFFFFF) across large expanses; surfaces default to calibrated light tints that lower optical strain.
- **Micro-tactile Precision:** High-fidelity borders, crisp line iconography, and ultra-smooth tactile depth indicating clinical refinement.
- **Breathing Space:** Expansive micro- and macro-spacing to simulate spacious visual focus and mitigate sensory overload.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is calibrated strictly for optical ease and WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance, prioritizing high contrast without eye-fatiguing glare.

- **Primary Deep Optical Blue (`#165B9F`):** Anchors primary brand actions, authoritative clinical callouts, and key interaction focal points. Minimum contrast ratio exceeds 4.8:1 against light canvas backdrops.
- **Dark Deep Ocean (`#0D477F`):** Used for intense functional states, primary headers, active selection outlines, and deep contrast anchors. Exceeds 7:1 against light surfaces.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Blue (`#2978B7`):** Secondary interactive elements, links, active state indicators, and subtle informational callouts.
- **Accent Cyan Aqua (`#83E2E9`):** Represents therapeutic hydration, cool temperature relief, and certified tech badges. Reserved for highlights, soft glow indicators, and active badge accents. Never used for raw unbacked body text.
- **Light Blue Wash (`#C1E1F9`):** Structural borders, soft dividers, badge surfaces, and low-contrast focus indicators.
- **Very Light Mist Canvas (`#F6FBFF`):** The primary soothing background tone designed to drastically reduce monitor luminescence compared to stark white.
- **Surface Elevation Neutral (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated interactive cards, floating modal containers, and checkout drawers to pop naturally against `#F6FBFF`.
- **Text & Neutral Scale:**
  - **Text High-Contrast:** `#0F2642` (Optimized deep navy for primary reading)
  - **Text Muted / Body Secondary:** `#425E7A`
  - **Text Subtle / Metadata:** `#6C88A3`
  - **Success / Botanical Safety:** `#128362`
  - **Warning / Optical Alert:** `#C0660A`
  - **Critical / Ocular Hazard:** `#BA2525`

### Functional Color Roles
- **Interactive Surfaces:** Hover and press states shift from `#165B9F` to `#0D477F`.
- **Cart & Conversion:** Checkout actions and primary CTA buttons use `#165B9F` with pure crisp `#FFFFFF` text to maintain an accessible, distraction-free purchasing highway.
- **Optical Tagging:** Blue-light products use blue shades (`#2978B7`), hydrating drops use light blue/cyan tones (`#83E2E9`), and soothing thermal masks use soft calming gradients.

## Typography

### Font Choice & Ocular Ergonomics
The design system deploys **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across all functional roles. Featuring wide apertures, crisp geometry, humanist warmth, and clean stroke termination, it is tailored for rapid comprehension and minimal saccadic eye strain during prolonged screen exposure.

### Typography Hierarchy & Usage Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for brand statements, campaign hero units, and category intros. Rendered with tight letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to guarantee compact visual cohesion.
- **Body Text:** Uses a relaxed line height ratio (1.5x–1.6x) to avoid line crowding. Characters remain open and distinct even at low screen contrast settings.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Used for pharmaceutical specifications, lens magnification ratios, thermal ratings, and status pills. Increased letter-spacing prevents visual bleed on dense e-commerce spec sheets.
- **Tabular Numerics:** For product pricing, prescription values (Diopters, Axis), and batch expiry counts, tabular numeric features (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) are enforced across all cart and technical tables.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy: Breathable Medical Commerce
The layout system treats negative space as an active functional component that calms visual tension. Cluttered multi-banner layouts are avoided in favor of structured, purposeful product grids and clear content sections.

### Grid Structure & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1360px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer margin `margin: 3rem`. Allows 4-column product cards, 3-column clinical articles, or 8/4 split cart and checkout architecture.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, dynamic outer margin `2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column checkout flows reflow to stacked layouts; product catalogs switch to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, outer screen margin `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Single or double card feeds with thumb-zone accessible action sheets.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Icon-to-text gaps, badge padding, internal chip spacing.
- **Component Spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Internal container padding for cards, forms, and product thumbnails.
- **Section Spacing (`space-xl` and multiples):** Generous vertical breathing gaps between marketing hero areas, trust indicator bars, and recommendation rails.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Soft-Light Elevation
This design system avoids harsh pitch-black drop shadows that create visual grit. Depth is conveyed using **luminescent cool-blue ambient shadows** combined with translucent borders, replicating the look of diffused optical glass and clinical daylight.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Default `#F6FBFF` Mist canvas. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Rest State):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 91, 159, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(13, 71, 127, 0.04);`
  Reinforced with a sub-perceptual border: `1px solid rgba(193, 225, 249, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive):**
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -4px rgba(22, 91, 159, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(131, 226, 233, 0.25);`
  The subtle cyan ambient under-glow communicates an active, soothing state.
- **Level 3 (Floating Sheets, Cart Drawers, Modals):**
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 71, 127, 0.18), 0 1px 2px rgba(13, 71, 127, 0.05);`
  Paired with a backdrop blur overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(13, 71, 127, 0.35)`).

### Tactile Boundary Precision
All elevated cards, modals, and sheets must feature an internal hairline rim or border using `#C1E1F9` or `rgba(193, 225, 249, 0.8)`. This keeps edges distinct against adjacent components without needing heavy, jarring borders.

## Shapes

### Curvature Geometry
The shape language uses moderate, soothing curvature that feels approachable and medically sound:
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, small tags, tooltips, and clinical spec indicators.
- **Primary Interactive & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** E-commerce product cards, review cards, cart items, and filter panels.
- **Hero Containers & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Promotional banners, prescription upload modals, and bottom sheets.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Category chips, dosage tags, status indicators ("In Stock", "Medical Grade"), and primary conversion buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Background `#165B9F`, label `#FFFFFF`, border none, shape `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (depending on card context). Hover shifts to `#0D477F`. Active scales to `0.98`. Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(131, 226, 233, 0.6)`.
- **Secondary (Prescription Setup / View Details):** Background `rgba(193, 225, 249, 0.35)`, text `#165B9F`, border `1px solid #C1E1F9`. Hover transitions to `#C1E1F9` background with `#0D477F` text.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#2978B7`, padding aligned to text. Underline accent on hover.

### 2. Product Cards
- **Base Surface:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(193, 225, 249, 0.7)`, radius `1.25rem` (20px), padding `space-md`.
- **Image Staging Area:** Soft light background `#F6FBFF` with rounded top corners, highlighting product clarity (lens anti-glare coatings, transparent eye drops, or gel textures).
- **Badge Integration:** Top-left floating pills for "Blue Light 99%", "Cold/Warm Therapy", "Preservative-Free". Badges use background `#F6FBFF`, text `#165B9F`, border `1px solid #83E2E9`.
- **Pricing & CTA Footers:** Pricing clearly formatted with tabular numerals; rapid one-click "Add" button integrated with an animated check state.

### 3. Form Inputs & Prescription Selectors
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #C1E1F9`, radius `0.75rem`. Text color `#0F2642`, placeholder color `#6C88A3`.
- **Focused State:** Border transitions to `#165B9F` with an outer glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(131, 226, 233, 0.45)`.
- **Stepper Inputs (Quantity / Diopter Power):** Unified segmented control with `#165B9F` interactive icons, bordered by `#C1E1F9`.

### 4. Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #C1E1F9`, text `#425E7A`, radius `rounded-full`, padding `0.5rem 1rem`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#165B9F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #165B9F`, accompanied by a subtle closing `x` icon.
- **Therapy Toggle Chips (Hot / Cold / Both):** Specialized chips with micro cyan/amber indicators that adapt based on the selected thermal mode.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** 20px × 20px, radius `6px`. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #C1E1F9`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: background `#165B9F`, border `#165B9F` with a white tick.
- **Radio Buttons:** 20px × 20px circle. Checked state features a concentric inner dot of `#165B9F` surrounded by white buffer space.
- **Prescription Toggle Switch:** 28px height, rounded pill. Active track in `#165B9F` with smooth tactile thumb slide.

### 6. Lists & Specification Tables
- **Clinical Spec Accordions:** Clean divider lines in `rgba(193, 225, 249, 0.5)`. Row padding `space-md 0`. Header utilizes `title-md` with subtle expand/collapse chevron animation.
- **Ingredient & Material Breakdown:** Alternating clean rows with clear typography pairings: property title in `#425E7A` and certified value in `#0F2642`.

### 7. Specialized Domain Components
- **Thermal Therapy Indicator:** Dual-state gauge card displaying optimal temperature ranges for hot (blepharitis, dry eye) and cold (puffiness, migraine relief) therapies.
- **Lens Coating Visualizer Modal:** Interactive card allowing shoppers to slide a filter across an image to preview anti-reflective, blue-cut, and UV400 clarity.
- **Optical Prescription Upload Vault:** Drop zone with dashed border in `#2978B7`, mist background `#F6FBFF`, and trusted medical encryption lock icon.
